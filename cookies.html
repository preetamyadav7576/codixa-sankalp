Cookie Policy

Codixa Sankalp Tech Solution is committed to providing a transparent and user-friendly experience. This Cookie Policy describes how we use cookies and related technologies to recognize visitors, improve website performance, and deliver a secure and personalized browsing experience. By continuing to use our website, you agree to the use of cookies as outlined below.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over time, making future visits faster, smoother, and more relevant to your needs.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to enhance usability, monitor performance, and continuously improve our digital services. Cookies help us:

  • Maintain secure sessions and website functionality
  • Understand visitor behavior and traffic patterns
  • Save user preferences and display settings
  • Provide relevant updates, notifications, or promotional information
  • Optimize website speed and performance

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following types of cookies to improve your browsing experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core website operations such as navigation, secure login, and form submissions. Without these, the website may not function properly.
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies: Collect anonymous statistical data to understand how users interact with the website, helping us enhance design, content quality, and usability.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember user selections like language, region, or interface preferences to deliver a customized experience.
  • Communication & Marketing Cookies: Used occasionally to provide relevant announcements, course updates, or promotional messages aligned with user interests.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow trusted third-party service providers such as analytics platforms, payment processors, or communication tools to place cookies on our website. These cookies are governed by the respective third parties’ privacy policies, and we encourage users to review them for further clarity.

5. Managing or Disabling Cookies

Users have full control over cookie settings through their web browser. You may:

  • Accept or reject cookies
  • Delete stored cookies
  • Set alerts before cookies are stored

Please note that disabling cookies may limit certain features, slow down performance, or reduce overall website functionality.

6. Data Privacy & Security

Cookies used by Codixa Sankalp Tech Solution do not store sensitive personal data such as passwords or financial details. All collected information is processed with appropriate security measures and used strictly for operational improvement, analytics, and user experience enhancement.

7. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update or modify this Cookie Policy at any time to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. Updated versions will be published on this page along with the revised effective date.
