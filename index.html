About Us

Welcome to Codixa Sankalp Tech Solution

Codixa Sankalp Tech Solution is a forward-thinking education and technology training organization dedicated to empowering students and learners with practical skills, modern technical knowledge, and realworld project experience. We focus on bridging the gap between academic learning and industry expectations by delivering hands-on training, mentorship, and career-oriented programs.

Our goal is to create confident, skilled, and job-ready professionals who can adapt to the fast-changing digital world. With experienced mentors, industry-relevant curriculum, and live project exposure, we ensure that every learner gains both technical expertise and professional growth.
